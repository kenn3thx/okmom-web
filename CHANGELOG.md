# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),

## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-27

### Added
- Privacy policy page at `/privacy` with full English content
- Terms of service page at `/terms` with full English content
- Legal documentation in `/docs` (privacy policy and terms of service in English)

### Changed
- Restructured legal page routes: `/privacy-policy` → `/privacy`, `/terms-of-service` → `/terms`
- Updated footer navigation links to point to new legal page routes
- Updated public header navigation to point to new legal page routes
- Updated navigation config with new route paths
- Updated GDPR compliance page references to new routes

### Removed
- Old `/privacy-policy` page (replaced by `/privacy`)
- Old `/terms-of-service` page (replaced by `/terms`)
