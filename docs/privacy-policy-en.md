# PRIVACY POLICY — OKMOM

**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

OKMOM is a mobile application that helps parents monitor their children's safety through GPS location tracking. The App is developed and operated by **OKMOM** ("we", "us"), which is responsible for controlling and processing your personal data.

**Data Controller:** OKMOM — determines the purposes and means of processing your personal data. For any privacy or data-related inquiries, please contact us at **privacy@okmom.co**.

This Privacy Policy applies to the OKMOM mobile application on Android and iOS platforms, as well as the website at [okmom.co](https://okmom.co) (the "Services").

Protecting your privacy and your child's privacy is our highest priority. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1. Parent Data

When you create an OKMOM account, we collect:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Display name | Identify your account |
| Email address | Login, verification, important notifications |
| Password (hashed) | Authentication |
| Avatar (optional) | Account personalization |
| Login method | Google, Apple, or Email |

### 2.2. Child Data

When you create a child profile in OKMOM, we collect:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Child's name | Identify the profile within the parent account |
| Gender (optional) | Personalize experience |
| Birth year (optional) | Determine the child's age |
| Avatar (optional) | Profile personalization |
| Star credits | Operate the reward system |

### 2.3. Location Data

This is the core feature of OKMOM. We collect:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Precise GPS location | Real-time location tracking of the child's device |
| Geofence status | Alerts when the child enters/leaves designated safe zones |
| Activity detection | Detect whether the child is walking or in a vehicle |

**Important notes:**
- Location is only collected from the **child's device**, after the parent has paired the device
- Location works **both when the app is open and when running in the background** to ensure continuous monitoring
- You can stop location collection at any time by disabling the location permission in your device's system Settings (Android: Settings → Apps → OKMOM → Permissions → Location; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Location Services → OKMOM)

### 2.4. Technical Data

We automatically collect certain technical information to ensure the service functions properly:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Unique device identifier (UUID, stored in Keystore/Keychain) | Distinguish devices within your account, support parent-child pairing |
| Battery level | Notify parents when the child's device is running low on battery |
| Connection type (WiFi / Mobile) | Optimize location update frequency |
| Device name (manufacturer + model, e.g., "Samsung Galaxy S24") | Set default device name in your account (you can change it later) |
| FCM token | Send push notifications to the parent's device |

### 2.5. Payment Data

When you subscribe to the Premium plan:

- Payments are processed **entirely through Google Play Store or Apple App Store**
- **OKMOM does not store, view, or process your credit card information**
- We only receive your subscription status (active / expired) from the app store

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

All collected data is used solely for the following purposes:

1. **Real-time child device location** — allows parents to view their child's current location on a map
2. **Geofence alerts** — sends notifications when a child enters or leaves safe zones set by the parent (home, school, etc.)
3. **Star reward system** — parents create tasks, children complete them to earn stars, accumulate stars to redeem rewards
4. **Safety alerts** — emergency notifications to parents
5. **Authentication and security** — protect your account from unauthorized access

**We DO NOT use data for:**
- ❌ Advertising in any form
- ❌ Tracking users across other apps/websites
- ❌ Selling or trading data with third parties
- ❌ Building marketing profiles

## 5. DATA SECURITY

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

| Measure | Details |
|---|---|
| **Encryption at rest** | AES-256 (Firebase Firestore, local SQLite, Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) |
| **Encryption in transit** | TLS 1.3 / HTTPS for all network connections |
| **Device authentication** | Firebase App Check — prevents access from spoofed devices |
| **Access control** | Only the paired parent account can view a child's data |
| **Passwords** | Hashed before storage — even we cannot read your original password |

In the event of a data breach, we will notify you via email and in-app notification within **72 hours** of discovery.

### 6.1. Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Parent account data | For the duration of the active account |
| Child profile data | For the duration of the parent's active account |
| Payment data | Per Google Play / Apple App Store policies |

### 6.2. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

1. Open the OKMOM app
2. Go to **Settings → Account & Security → Close my account**
3. Confirm the deletion request

After confirmation:
- Your data will be scheduled for deletion
- Deletion is completed within **30 days**
- During this 30-day period, if you change your mind, simply **log back in** to your account and select "Cancel deletion request". Your account and all data will be restored immediately.

**Note on child data:** When you delete your account, all child profile data linked to your account will also be permanently deleted.

### 6.3. Exceptions

In certain cases, we may retain data longer if:
- There is a legal obligation (as required by competent authorities)
- It is necessary for security and fraud prevention
- The data has been fully anonymized (cannot identify an individual)

## 9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

OKMOM is designed for parents to monitor their own children. We are especially strict about children's data:

- **Parental consent:** When pairing a child's device, you confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian and consent to the collection of the child's location data for safety monitoring purposes.
- **Children cannot create accounts:** Only adults (≥18 years old) can create parent accounts.
- **Data minimization:** We only collect child data necessary for monitoring features — no excessive collection.
- **Device identifiers:** The UUID used in OKMOM is an app-generated identifier stored in Keystore/Keychain, **not** a system identifier (AAID, IMEI, MAC, Serial Number). We do not collect persistent system identifiers from children's devices.
- **Platform compliance:** OKMOM complies with **Google Play Families Policy** and **Apple App Store Kids Category** requirements.

We comply with international children's privacy regulations:

| Region | Regulation | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **United States** | COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) | Parental consent required for children under 13 |
| **Europe** | GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) | Parental consent required for children under 16 |

If you discover that a child's data has been collected without parental consent, please contact **privacy@okmom.co** immediately so we can delete that data.

## 10. DEVICE SYSTEM PERMISSIONS

To provide location monitoring features, OKMOM requires the following system permissions. Each permission has a specific purpose and is only used for its stated function:

### Android

| Permission | Why Needed |
|---|---|
| Precise location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) | Accurate GPS location of the child's device |
| Approximate location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) | WiFi/cellular positioning when GPS is weak |
| Background location (`ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`) | Continue location tracking when the app is closed |
| Activity recognition (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`) | Detect whether the child is walking or in a vehicle |
| Boot completed (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | Automatically restart monitoring after device reboot |
| Foreground service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`) | Maintain continuous location monitoring |
| Foreground service - location (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`) | Allow foreground service to access location |
| Foreground service - data sync (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC`) | Allow foreground service to sync data |
| Ignore battery optimizations (`REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`) | Prevent system from killing monitoring to save battery |
| Wake lock (`WAKE_LOCK`) | Maintain connection for timely location updates |
| Exact alarm (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`) | Schedule periodic location checks |
| Vibrate (`VIBRATE`) | Vibration for alerts |
| Internet (`INTERNET`) | Network connection for data sync |

### iOS

| Permission | Why Needed |
|---|---|
| Location "Always" (`NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription`) | Continuous location even when the app is closed |
| Location "When in use" (`NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`) | Location when the app is open |
| Background modes (`location`, `fetch`, `processing`) | Location updates and data sync in background |
